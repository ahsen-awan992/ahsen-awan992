# Custom CRM + ERP Platform Driving 52% Revenue Growth

**Role:** Product Owner / Director of Products & Projects, Teralight Gateway Holdings Inc.
**Skills:** CRM · ERP · Custom Software · Business Intelligence · Team Leadership (20+ developers, designers, QA)

## The problem
The company had outgrown its off-the-shelf tools. The CRM could not handle their sales workflow, the ERP was a patchwork of spreadsheets and disconnected apps, and leadership was deciding on incomplete data.

## What I did
As Product Owner (SAFe and Scrum), I designed and led delivery of a unified platform from scratch: a custom CRM tailored to their exact sales process, an ERP that automated operations, and executive dashboards giving leadership real-time visibility.

## The outcome
- **52.3% average annual revenue increase**
- **20% average market-share growth**
- **15% increase** in customer satisfaction, **10% reduction** in time-to-market

## The takeaway
Those results came from finally giving the business the right tools and the right data — not from any single feature.

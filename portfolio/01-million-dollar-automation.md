# The Million-Dollar Automation

**Role:** Director of Products & Projects, Teralight Gateway Holdings Inc. (Vancouver, Canada)
**Skills:** Process Automation · Product Strategy · Software Architecture · Technical Team Leadership

## The problem
A core business process was entirely manual. The team spent hundreds of hours a year on repetitive, error-prone tasks, and the cost scaled with every new client.

## What I did
I analyzed the workflow end to end, mapped every bottleneck, and led the design of a system that automated the full pipeline. We did not build a one-off script — we built a product: reliable, scalable, and maintainable enough to stand on its own, using a proper SDLC blending waterfall and agile.

## The outcome
- The automated system became a **standalone product valued at over $1M**
- **25% improvement** in project delivery efficiency
- **10.2% reduction** in project timelines

## The takeaway
The best software does not just solve a problem — it becomes more valuable than the problem it replaced.
